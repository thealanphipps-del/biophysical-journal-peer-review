## 2. Two Separate Evidence Tracks

### Track 1: Software Claims (Certified by Testing — `523 / 523` Tests Passing)
The software platform successfully passed its defined verification, integrity, reproducibility, provenance, and adversarial test suites and operates according to the documented specification:
1. SHA-256 manifest verification and cryptographic file-lock enforcement detect any unauthorized modifications.
2. Merkle provenance chains (`L1 -> L7`) reconstruct deterministically under RFC 8785 canonical JSON serialization.
3. `RULE-VI-26` deterministic replay reproduces identical outputs and hashes ($\Delta \le 10^{-12}$) from identical `L1` inputs.
4. `L6` Court-of-Admissibility gates (`Gate A` through `Gate E`) execute according to specification.
5. Audit logs, chain-of-custody records, and adversarial test cases are handled as specified.
6. Repository state is reproducible from tagged release `v4.0.0-court-authority`.

### Track 2: Scientific Claims (Require Independent Empirical Validation)
The system computes and reports candidate correspondences according to the implemented mathematical and statistical framework. The biological validity of those correspondences remains an empirical question requiring independent experimental investigation. Software testing alone does **not** prove:
- That a specific acoustic frequency corresponds to a biological molecule in vivo.
- That a spectral peak identifies a protein in vivo.
- That an octave projection ($\log_2(X) \bmod 1$, where single-octave variance collapses $1,341\times$ to $R^2 = 0.0006$ and Meyer 319-compound window saturation reaches $68.39\%$) has biological meaning.
- That human health conditions can be diagnosed from detected acoustic frequencies.
